What to include

Include the app name, app version, iOS version and a clear description of the issue. Screenshots may be included when they do not expose sensitive information.

Do not send passwords, payment information or other unnecessary sensitive data.

Privacy and data requests

Privacy questions and valid access or deletion requests can be sent to the same support email. Identity verification may be required before a request is completed.

Before contacting support

Update the app and iOS, restart the app, confirm available storage, and repeat the action that caused the issue.
